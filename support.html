Support

Straight answers to the questions coop-keepers actually ask. Anything else — email, a person reads it.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync?

Everything lives on your device. If you're signed into iCloud, EggBasket also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud (CloudKit), which syncs your records across your iPhone and iPad automatically. That iCloud space belongs to your Apple Account — we run no servers and cannot see it.

How do I back up or restore my records?

Two layers, both free:

  • iCloud sync — sign into the same Apple Account on a new phone, install EggBasket, and your flock's history comes back on its own.
  • Backup file — in Settings you can export a backup file of everything, keep it wherever you like (Files, a drive, an email to yourself), and re-import it later on any device.

I bought Pro on my old phone. Do I pay again?

No. The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. On the new device open Settings → Restore Purchases and it comes back at no charge. Family Sharing follows Apple's rules for shared purchases.

How many hens or flocks can I track?

The free app covers a typical backyard setup — one flock, with daily counts, notes, and history. Pro removes the limits: multiple flocks and coops, per-hen records, and the full cost-per-egg toolkit. Current specifics are always listed on the upgrade screen inside the app.

Can I export my records?

Yes — CSV export gets your laying records and expenses out as a plain spreadsheet file that opens in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets. Your data is never locked in.

Something's broken, or I have an idea

Email is the fastest route. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when it went sideways; screenshots help. Feature ideas from working coop-keepers shape what gets built next.
