Privacy Policy

Effective 28 July 2026 · Applies to EggBasket: Chicken Egg Tracker for iOS

The short version: we collect nothing. EggBasket has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of ours. Your flock's records stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

What EggBasket stores

Egg counts, flock and hen details, expenses, and notes you enter. All of it is stored on your device. That's the only place we put it.

iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, EggBasket syncs your records through Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud database. That database is tied to your Apple Account, protected by Apple, and technically inaccessible to us — we cannot read, browse, or recover it. Turning off iCloud for EggBasket in iOS Settings stops sync at any time.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. You never register, so there is no profile of you to hold.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party trackers.
  • No data sales or sharing. We have nothing to sell or share.
  • No network calls to us. EggBasket works fully offline; there is no server of ours for it to talk to.

Notifications

If you set a collection reminder, it's a local notification scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing about it leaves your phone.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and gives us no personal or payment details about you. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Backups and export

Backup files and CSV exports are created on your device at your request and saved wherever you choose. Where you send them is up to you.

Email support

If you email us, we see your address and what you wrote — used only to reply, kept only as ordinary correspondence, never added to any marketing list.

The formal bits

Data controller: Shakti Thakran (independent developer), reachable at shakti.thakran@outlook.com.

Personal data processed: none is transmitted to or held by the developer. App content resides in on-device storage and, optionally, the user's private CloudKit database, processed by Apple Inc. as described in Apple's iCloud terms.

Legal basis (GDPR, where applicable): not applicable to the developer, as no personal data is collected; support email is handled under legitimate interest in responding to your request.

Children: EggBasket collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights: your data is already in your hands — view it in the app, export it via CSV or backup file, or erase it by deleting the app's data and its iCloud storage (iOS Settings → your Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage).

Changes: if a future version of the app changes what is stored or synced, this policy will be updated here first, with a new effective date.
